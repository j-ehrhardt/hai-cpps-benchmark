Fault Proxies and Diagnosis

HAI-CPPS v2 uses configured component behaviours as fault proxies. A generated fault scenario activates one proxy in one module; the campaign generator creates and validates the corresponding normal reference run as well.

Fault onset is configured per topology in benchmark_setup.json and recorded in fault_events.json and technical_timing.json. The fault metadata identifies the target module and proxy. It is an offline evaluation artifact, not an online measurement feature.

The available proxy depends on the module type:

  • all process modules: anom_leaking, anom_valve_in0, anom_pump50, and anom_pump75 where applicable;

  • mixer modules additionally support anom_valve_in1 and anom_valve_in2;

  • filter modules additionally support anom_pollution; and

  • distillation modules additionally support anom_heat50 and anom_heat75.

The 50% and 75% variants of the same pump or heater cannot be active together.

Leaking Anomaly — anom_leaking

When anom_leaking is active, a leaking valve opens between tanks in the affected module. Fluid leaves the process plant through a separate sink.

Typical observable effects can include:

  • reduced pressure in downstream pipes or tanks;

  • lower volume-flow rates in the affected module or its neighbours;

  • decreasing tank levels despite unchanged control signals; and

  • an imbalance between inflow and outflow rates.

The measurement exports exclude simulator-internal leakage states. Use a measurement view, rather than oracle states or metadata, as the input to an online model.

Filter Pollution Anomaly — anom_pollution

This proxy is specific to the Filter module. It changes the filter-pollution behaviour after fault onset, affecting the pressure difference and flow through the filter.

Pump Lower Performance — anom_pump75 and anom_pump50

These proxies reduce pump performance to 75% or 50% of nominal performance. They can lead to lower flow, reduced pressure, and longer tank-filling times.

Only one pump-performance proxy may be active in a scenario.

Inlet Valve Anomaly — anom_valve_inX

An inlet valve proxy prevents the specified inlet valve from fully closing. The valve remains partly open, allowing unintended fluid inflow when the module is not in its intended filling state. The mixer can target in0, in1, or in2; the other supported process modules expose in0.

Heater Lower Performance — anom_heat75 and anom_heat50

These proxies are specific to the Distill module and reduce the distillation heater to 75% or 50% of nominal heat input. Only one heater-performance proxy may be active in a scenario.
