Welcome to the Documentation of HAI-CPPS v2.2
=============================================

The **Hamburg AI Benchmark for Cyber-Physical Production Systems (HAI-CPPS)** is a benchmark for evaluating AI methods in anomaly detection, diagnosis, reconfiguration, and planning for modular cyber-physical process plants.

HAI-CPPS comprises ten process-plant topologies with increasing complexity. The v2.2 campaign contains 350 recordings: 160 healthy and 190 recordings with one enabled fault proxy in one module. Each fault has persistent and temporary variants paired with a seed-matched healthy test control.

.. image:: /_static/imgs/banner.png
   :alt: HAI-CPPS process plant
   :class: align-center
   :width: 100%

HAI-CPPS includes:

- OpenModelica simulation models
- Published benchmark datasets
- Validated generation, export, and release checks
- A parallel campaign launcher with per-topology manifests and frozen generation source

The release separates online measurements and recorded controller commands from simulator-derived oracle states and offline metadata. You can also simulate a custom topology or module and obtain a raw OpenModelica CSV.

.. note::

   **HAI-CPPS** extends the **Benchmark for Diagnosis, Reconfiguration, and Planning (BeRfiPl)**.
   You can access the previous version here:
   `BeRfiPl Benchmark <https://github.com/j-ehrhardt/benchmark-for-diagnosis-reconf-planning/tree/benchmark_v1>`_

    doi = {10.21227/5ewb-cn40},
